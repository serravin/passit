# PassIt — Monetization

PassIt uses a **freemium model**: the core social game is free, with optional paid features for personalization, AI assistance and hosting more customized games. The following are proposed revenue streams; pricing and usage limits remain to be validated.

### Free Core Experience

Users can create and join Chains, form groups with friends or through random selection, contribute, pass, publish, discover and like stories for free. Automatic titles and system-assigned Motives are included in the core game and do not consume paid writing-suggestion credits. A baseline set of AI suggestions for every turn, including its timeout fallback, is included in the core game.

### PassIt Plus — Subscription

An optional subscription offers:

- **More AI assistance:** additional on-demand continuation suggestions and setup assistance. Extra suggestions do not replace the baseline timeout fallback. Automatic Chain titles remain free.
- **Advanced host settings:** additional customization, such as larger groups within the global cap. Assigned Motives remain controlled by the system.
- **Story presentation:** premium themes and export styles for finished Chains.
- **Personalization:** exclusive profile and Chain cosmetics.

Paid host features apply to the hosted Chain; participants do not need a subscription to take part. Basic settings, including the turn timeout and the minimum participant count, remain free. Automatic AI timeout contributions are part of the core game and do not depend on a participant's paid suggestion allowance.

### One-Time Purchases

Users can buy optional themed Motive packs, Chain covers, profile cosmetics or additional AI credits without subscribing. Packs could focus on themes such as awkward dates, workplace comedy or absurd mysteries. Standard Motives remain free.

### Sponsored Challenges

Brands can sponsor clearly labeled comedy storytelling challenges with a themed setup or eligible Motive pack. Users voluntarily create Chains around the challenge, using the same group, direct participation, system-led Motive assignment and random-pass rules as any other Chain.

Sponsorship provides a branded challenge placement in Discover; it does not buy organic likes or a higher organic ranking.

### Monetization Principles & Rollout

- Keep participation and reading finished stories free.
- Paid features do not influence random participant selection, turn order or organic rankings.
- Keep monetization outside the active writing and passing flow.
- Start by validating repeat play and completed Chains, then test PassIt Plus and cosmetics. Introduce sponsored challenges once there is an active audience.

## Monetization Entities

Potential monetization entities include **Subscription**, **Product**, **Purchase**, **AI Credit Transaction** and **Sponsored Challenge**. AI credit accounting can record usage without retaining suggestion content.
